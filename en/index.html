Frequently asked questions

Do I need a subscription?

The app is free. Support via CloudTips is optional.

Do I need an installer?

Download the full Windows ZIP, extract it and run DayZCompanion.exe. First-launch guide.

Where can I check compatibility?

See the download page for general requirements. Individual tools may have additional requirements.

Where can I report a bug?

Use the Yandex form. Include your Companion version and describe what happened before the error.

How do I use Morse / Nasdara?

Tune the radio, start listening and wait for the decoded result. Morse / Nasdara guide.

What should I consider when using Companion on DayZ servers?

Check the rules of your chosen server: its administrators may restrict individual tools. More about server rules.
