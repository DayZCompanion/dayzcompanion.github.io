Features

Nine tools. One app.

Access all your tools and settings in one place instead of juggling separate utilities.

Extra Zoom (Magnifier)

Magnify the center of the screen for a closer look at your target.

More details ↗

Uses Windows screen magnification to enlarge a circular area in the center of your display. Take a closer look at distant targets and small details without changing the optics in DayZ.

Morse / Nasdara

Decode Morse signals into bunker coordinates and an access code.

More details ↗

Listens to DayZ audio and decodes Morse signals on Nasdara. A small in-game overlay displays the incoming signals and digits as they are decoded. When the message is complete, it shows the bunker’s X/Y coordinates, PIN code, and how many times the message was repeated. Results are saved in the history.

Gamma

Brighten the image to see more clearly during DayZ’s darkest nights.

More details ↗

Nights in DayZ can be so dark that the environment is hard to make out. Adjust gamma to better see terrain, buildings, and landmarks without repeatedly changing your monitor settings.

Earplugs

Turn down loud DayZ sounds so you can hear your friends on Discord.

More details ↗

Engines, vehicles, and other loud in-game sounds can drown out voice chat. Earplugs quickly reduce DayZ’s volume without turning down Discord or other apps, making team communication easier.

Auto-run

Travel long distances without holding down movement keys.

More details ↗

DayZ’s large maps mean long journeys. Enable auto-run to keep your character moving without holding a key. Steer with your mouse and adjust your route as needed.

On-screen Crosshair

A customizable dot or crosshair in the center of your screen.

More details ↗

Displays a small aiming marker over the game — either a dot or a crosshair. Adjust its shape, color, size, and position to suit your preference.

Code Lock

Enter a six-digit lock code automatically with one hotkey.

More details ↗

Save your six-digit code and assign a hotkey. When you approach the lock, Companion types the combination and presses Enter. No need to remember and retype a long code every time.

Maps

DayZ maps to help you navigate the game world.

More details ↗

Explore game location maps and use them to find your way around DayZ.

Auto-lean

Automatically lean left or right while moving sideways.

More details ↗

Press A or D to make your character lean left or right accordingly. This can help when peeking around corners and moving near cover.

Frequently Asked Questions

About DayZ Companion

A few essentials about how the app works and what to know before using it.

Is DayZ Companion free?

Yes. The app is free, with no subscriptions or paid tools. If it has been helpful, you can choose to support its development.

Do I need to install DayZ mods?

No. Companion runs separately from DayZ and does not require in-game mods. You can enable and configure its tools in the app.

Does Companion modify DayZ files?

Companion runs as a separate Windows application and does not modify DayZ game assets or executable files. Its tools use Windows features to work with audio and visuals, display overlays, and send keyboard input.

Can I use these tools on any server?

Server rules vary. Some communities restrict third-party software and automation. Check your server’s rules before using these tools.
