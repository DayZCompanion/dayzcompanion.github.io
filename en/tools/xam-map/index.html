Frequently asked questions

How does the map appear over the game?

The map opens in a separate window over the game, so you do not need to switch to an external browser. Internet access is required to load the maps.

How do I find a bunker?

The Morse decoder includes a built-in Nasdara bunker map. To search the interactive map, double-click a Morse history entry to copy its coordinates.

Can I open a map without running the game?

Yes, open the map from DayZ Companion.

How do I close the map?

Press the assigned hotkey (M by default), Esc or the window’s close button.
