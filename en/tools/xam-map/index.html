Features

  • Opens the XAM service

    The independent XAM project supplies the maps and their content. Open the original XAM site.

  • Helps with navigation

    XAM offers Chernarus, Livonia, Sakhal and Nasdara maps. Choose the world your server uses.

Companion interface

Open the screenshot to take a closer look at the interface.

How to open a map

  1. Choose Maps

    Open the maps section in the Companion control centre.

  2. Open XAM

    Open the third-party service and select your world.

  3. Find landmarks

    Use the map for your world and the layers available on the site.

How it works

  • Attribution and availability

    The XAM project maintains XAM Maps. It determines the available worlds, layers and service availability.

  • Connection

    Opening XAM requires access to its website.

Nasdara bunker map

Companion includes a separate Nasdara bunker map for getting around the underground complex. The Nasdara radio decoder handles digital transmissions separately.
