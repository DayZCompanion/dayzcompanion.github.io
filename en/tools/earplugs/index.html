Earplugs for DayZ

Turn DayZ down temporarily during loud moments in the game.

Go to the guideView demo
Two volume levels

Switch between your usual volume and a quieter level you choose.

FeaturesDemoGuideHow it worksQuestions

Features

  • Lowers game audio

    Set how far you want to reduce the volume.

  • Restores the usual level

    Toggle the hotkey again to return to your regular volume.

Companion interface

Open the screenshot to take a closer look at the interface.

How to use earplugs

  1. Start DayZ

    Check that game audio is playing.

  2. Set the volume

    Open Earplugs in Companion and assign a key.

  3. Try both levels

    In the game, turn on the quieter level and switch back.

How it works

  • Windows audio session

    Companion adjusts the game’s audio session volume.

  • Running game

    The DayZ audio session must be available when you toggle the setting.

Frequently asked questions

What if the game stays at the same volume?

Check that DayZ is playing sound, Companion can see it and no other action uses the shortcut.

Can earplugs reduce individual gunshots?

The volume change applies to the whole game audio session.
