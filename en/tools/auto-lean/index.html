Auto Lean for DayZ

Automatically lean left or right when you strafe in DayZ.

Download DayZ Companion

Links movement to leaning

In Auto Lean, A triggers Q and D triggers E. A and D together return to neutral.

Toggles with a hotkey

Assign a hotkey to turn automatic leaning on and off.

Manual controls remain available

Use Q and E to lean manually. Companion releases the keys it holds when you disable the module or switch away from DayZ.

How to set up

  1. Check the game controls

    Check that A and D strafe, and Q and E lean, in DayZ.

  2. Set up Companion

    Open Auto Lean, enable the tool and assign its toggle key.

  3. Check it in DayZ

    Try moving left and right. To turn the tool off, press the hotkey you assigned earlier.

Frequently asked questions

How does Auto Lean work?

Companion sends keyboard input through Windows: A triggers Q, D triggers E. A and D together return to neutral. DayZ must be focused; check the server rules.

Why does only one side work?

Check that the default DayZ bindings for strafing and leaning are active.

Can I still lean manually?

Yes. Q and E are not blocked. Use them normally when not strafing left or right, or disable the tool to restore normal controls.

Does Shift affect Auto Lean?

No. Automatic leaning depends on A and D. When the module is disabled or DayZ loses focus, Companion releases the lean keys it holds.
