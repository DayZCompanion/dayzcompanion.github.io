Features

  • Enlarges part of the frame

    The magnifier shows a scaled crop of the rendered image in a separate view.

  • Works with a hotkey

    Assign a key to open and close the magnifier while playing.

Companion interface

Open the screenshot to take a closer look at the interface.

How to set up the magnifier

  1. Open the magnifier settings

    Choose Magnifier in the control centre.

  2. Set the controls

    Adjust the zoom level and assign a convenient hotkey.

  3. Check the image

    Return to DayZ in windowed mode and turn on the magnifier.

How it works

  • Screen capture

    The tool captures a visible part of the image and scales it in a separate window.

  • Window mode

    Capture or overlay display may be unavailable in exclusive fullscreen.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the magnifier black?

Try switching DayZ to windowed or borderless windowed mode, then toggle the magnifier again.

Does it change the DayZ field of view?

Field of view stays a game setting. The magnifier enlarges a crop of the captured frame.
