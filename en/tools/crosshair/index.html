Features

  • Draws a marker over the image

    The marker is drawn in a separate overlay.

  • Flexible crosshair settings

    Choose a dot or cross, size, thickness and colour. Adjust its horizontal and vertical position and check the preview.

How to enable the marker

  1. Open Crosshair

    Select the tool in the control centre.

  2. Set the appearance

    Choose your marker settings and check that it is easy to see.

  3. Test the overlay

    Enable it with DayZ in borderless windowed mode.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I see the crosshair?

Check that the tool is enabled and DayZ is in windowed or borderless windowed mode.

Does the marker change how aiming works?

The marker is a visual reference. Ballistics and aiming remain part of the game.

Can I change the crosshair shape and colour?

Yes. Choose the shape, size, thickness and colour in the tool settings.

How is the crosshair displayed?

Windows displays it as a separate overlay. Exclusive fullscreen may hide it. Check the server rules.

Can I adjust the crosshair position?

Yes. Offset the position by up to ±20 pixels on each axis or reset it to the centre.

What happens when I open the map?

The crosshair hides automatically when the Companion map opens or DayZ loses focus.
