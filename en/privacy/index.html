Privacy policy

Information about data processing when visiting dayz-mod.ru.

1. Scope

This policy describes the processing of personal data of visitors to the DayZ Companion website (dayz-mod.ru) and users of the feedback form. The website does not require registration. Data processing within the Windows app is outside the scope of this document.

The personal data controller is the individual who owns and administers the DayZ Companion project and the dayz-mod.ru website. Contact the controller using the feedback form.

2. Website visits

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

3. Analytics and cookies

The website uses Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC), including Session Replay and click maps, to understand traffic and improve the website. The service receives device and browser information, IP addresses, pages visited, referral sources, interactions and technical identifiers. Clicks on download, feedback and donation links are counted; download events include the build version. Session Replay records website interactions; recordings are available for 15 days, including the current day. Data collected by Metrica.

Metrica uses cookies and localStorage. The website also remembers dismissal of its cookie notice in localStorage. Dismissing the notice does not turn off analytics. Browser settings allow you to restrict and delete cookies and site data and block the counter. See Metrica cookies and the Metrica terms.

4. Feedback

Requests are submitted via an external Yandex form. Message content, contact details if provided, and optional diagnostic archives or screenshots are processed to respond to requests, investigate issues and improve the app. Responses are stored in Yandex Forms; copies may also be delivered to the controller’s email account. The specified retention period for requests and attachments is no more than three months from receipt, unless a different period is required by law. Do not submit passwords, keys, payment credentials or other people’s personal data. Yandex privacy policy.

5. External services

Downloads use GitHub Releases; voluntary donations use CloudTips. The website has no card payment fields: payment information is entered on the payment provider’s website. Original XAM interactive maps are accessed via an external link. External services process data under their own rules: GitHub, CloudTips, Yandex.

6. Rights and requests

You have the right to request information about processing of your personal data, correction, blocking or deletion where legally justified, withdrawal of consent and cessation of processing, and to complain to the competent Russian data protection authority or a court. Use the feedback form and identify your request as a personal data enquiry.

7. Processing and retention

Processing is limited to data necessary for the stated purposes. When the retention period expires or other legal grounds for ending processing arise, personal data must be deleted or anonymised, including copies of requests in email.
